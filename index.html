ECI seeks to remove half the Bengal voters judges cleared

The Election Commission has appealed against 14.76 lakh voters whom judicial officers found eligible in the SIR. The weight falls hardest on the Muslim-majority border seats of Malda, Murshidabad and Uttar Dinajpur.

Same rate, very different weight

The ECI appealed against roughly half of the voters judges cleared, whatever the make-up of the seat. But judges cleared far more people in Muslim-majority seats, so the appeals add up to a much larger share of their voters.

Against the judges' own verdicts

Judges marked 27.16 lakh voters not eligible, 45% of everyone placed under adjudication. If every ECI appeal succeeded, another 14.76 lakh would go, taking the total to nearly 70%. In 83 seats, the ECI is seeking to remove more voters than the judges did.
